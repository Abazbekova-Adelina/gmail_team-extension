Threadly

Discuss this email privately with your team.

A private team chat attached to every Gmail thread. Create a workspace, invite teammates, and each email gets its own discussion. Gemini turns that discussion into a reply draft.

Workspace invitation

Install in Chrome

Download extension
  1. Unzip the download into a folder you can keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Open Threadly from Chrome's extensions menu and sign in with Google. Reload Gmail and open a thread.
  5. Create a workspace or accept your invitation in Workspace settings.

Downloads will be available once Google sign-in is ready.
