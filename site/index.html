Gmail Team Chat

Discuss email with your team, right below the conversation in Gmail. Create a private workspace, invite teammates, and keep a separate discussion for each thread.

Install in Chrome

Download extension
  1. Unzip the download into a folder you can keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Reload Gmail, open a thread, and sign in with Google.
  5. Create a workspace or accept your invitation in Manage team.

Downloads will be available once Google sign-in is ready.
